Across every screen

  • NewOne figure style for every number that matters. It is Archivo, the font you already load, set wider and heavier.
  • ClearerColour carries meaning and nothing else. Green is gained, red is wasted, amber is unused and blue is clickable.
  • ClearerFewer boxes. The lead figure sits on the page, and panels only hold lists and charts.
  • SwapNumbers in tables and charts drop the monospace face for tabular Archivo.
  • ShorterLabels stay under six words. Anything longer moves into a tooltip or a fold.
  • NewThe header turns translucent, so the page scrolls under it.

What stays the same

  • The light and dark colours you just restored.
  • Archivo and IBM Plex Sans.
  • Every number, and the rules that produce it.
  • Every change to graph8, with its confirmation and undo.
  • The period switch, Sync now, the demo-data pill and the phone layout.

Worth checking before the demo

  • The planner forecasts about 19 meetings from verifying 117 Starter list contacts, roughly 6 credits a meeting. The same page calls the Starter list your costliest at 1,233 a meeting. The forecast uses a per-contact rate from the simulated contacts, so a judge may ask about the gap.
  • The trend figure moves during the day because its weeks count back from now. It read down 32% at 13:26 and down 35% at 14:31 UTC. Quote it from the screen, not from the script.
