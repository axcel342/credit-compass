Rules that keep it from being spam
  • One home: every finding lives in the dashboard; nothing exists only as a notification.
  • Interrupt only when it's worth money: a spend-time nudge appears only if the estimated saving is material (e.g. >15% or >500 credits) and there's enough history to be confident.
  • Weekly, capped, skippable: at most 3 items, and no recap at all in a quiet week. In-app by default; Slack or email are opt-in.
  • Show the evidence: every finding says what it's based on ("17 meetings, 2,100 credits") plus a confidence level.
  • Never acts alone: one-click actions open a preview with the cost first, using graph8's existing dry-run and confirm step.
  • Learns from dismissals: a snoozed finding type is shown less often.
